## The park record (`world_parks`)

Node title plus these fields:

| Field | Type | Stored in | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| field_park_name | text | content_type_world_parks | probably duplicates the title, check |
| field_park_description | textarea | content_type_world_parks | the body in node_revisions may be the one actually shown, check |
| field_pactivities | text, multi-value, select list | content_field_pactivities | ~3.2 per park; has an extra custom column `activity_images` |
| field_pownership | text | content_type_world_parks | free text, e.g. "City of Salisbury" |
| field_pacreage | formatted_decimal | content_type_world_parks, `decimal(20,2)` | this is the acre counter's source |
| field_parkweb | text | content_type_world_parks | park website |
| field_parkimage | image, multi-value | content_field_parkimage | 68,986 rows but only 2,726 files exist, so most rows are empty |
| location | location module | location_instance -> location | name, street, city, province, postal, country, lat, lon |

Where the places are: 67,096 in the US, 115 Canada, 35 Spain, 35 Mexico, then a long tail across 55 countries totalling about 370. "World" coverage is real but thin. 143 US rows have a blank state.

Biggest states: CA 5,137, IL 4,774, MN 4,542, FL 3,349, MA 3,314, NY 3,283, TX 3,032.

## The "Park Tags" vocabulary is a hidden index, not tags

49,746 terms, free-tagging. The most-used ones are all of the shape **City State Facility**: "Chicago Illinois Parks", "Houston Texas Playgrounds", "Sacramento California Picnic Areas", "Brooklyn New York Basketball Courts", "Queens New York Spraygrounds", plus state-level ones like "Minnesota Waterfowl Production Areas" and "California State Parks".

Each term also has its own URL: 50,309 aliases start with `category/`, one per term. These are the site's real browse pages and some will have search rankings.

In WordPress this should not be 50,000 tags. It splits into two things we already have or can derive: the place hierarchy (city, state) and a **facility type** vocabulary (park, playground, basketball court, picnic area, tennis court, baseball field, softball field, soccer field, handball court, sprayground, volleyball court, wildlife management area, waterfowl production area, state park, state forest, game lands ...). The importer parses each term into place + facility, and the old `category/...` URLs redirect to the new city-and-facility pages.

## The photo contest, as built

`photo_contest` nodes, 254 of them:

| Field | Type |
|---|---|
| field_photo_contest | one image |
| field_contest_star_rating | integer |
| field_winning_voter | integer (a user id?) |
| field_contest_codings | textarea, "contest coding" |
| field_contest_comment | text |
| Contest Tags vocabulary | 421 terms |

Voting is Fivestar on the photo node: 16,084 votes across 256 items by 77 voters, stored as a percent. Many photos were sourced from Flickr (filenames like `40019179995_1f285d910d_c.jpg`), which matters for who may republish them.

`upload_park_picture` is a second, separate member-upload feature: a photo tied to a park by node reference and to the uploader by user reference. 59 of them, last used 2017. In the new site this and the contest become one thing: a photo pinned to a park, optionally entered in a contest.

## Members

757 accounts, 444 active, **1 signed in during the last year** (the owner), 3 in the last three years. Roles: 5 Site_Admin, 3 Site_Owner, everyone else plain. A visible share of registrations are spam (`out-mail.com`, `.ru` addresses, gibberish names).

Recommendation: do not migrate accounts. Start registration fresh. Carry over only the display names of the 77 voters and the photo uploaders, for attribution on their photos.

## Small or dead

- Comments: 42 in total across all types. Migrate the park and blog ones, skip the rest.
- Forum: 11 topics, 3 of them Spanish spam. Two are the old contest rules ("Photo rules", "Park Creation Contest"), worth keeping as text. Dropped, as agreed.
- Blog: 80 posts by a handful of authors, mostly the owner. 193 blog tags. Migrates to normal posts.
- Pages: 11, several are placeholders for the old finder ("Search Results", "City", "Parks", "Try Again!"). Only About Us has content.
- `parks` table, 16,947 rows, with `migrate_map_world_parks_2`: the source of the original 2010 import. Historical, not needed.
- `location_old`, 38,380 rows: leftover from an older location layout. Not needed.
- `nodewords` (6,166 rows): hand-written meta descriptions on some nodes. Worth carrying into the new SEO fields.
- `weather` module tables: weather per location was tried once. Not in scope.

## URL aliases that need care

- 75 aliases contain the literal text `[location-province_0]`: a Pathauto token that never got replaced. Those pages have broken URLs today and should get proper ones plus a redirect.
- A few hundred depth-2 aliases start with a state or province code (`ca/...`, `bc/...`, `on/...`, `madrid/...`, `chp/...`): parks with no city, and non-US places. Their new URLs will differ, so they need redirects.
- 612 user aliases in three depths: dropped with the accounts.

## The park records themselves (from the full query 3 export, 67,435 published parks)

Analysed offline with `analyze_parks.py` and `analyze_parks2.py`.

**The acre counter.** Summed from the database: **4,744,108,374.74**, within 3,217 acres of what the site shows, so the site's figure is a live sum and the column is clean. But the total is dominated by a handful of rows:

| Slice | Acres | Share |
|---|---|---|
| "Continent of Antarctica", one record | 3,459,475,340.54 | 72.9% |
| United States, all records | 828,975,859.79 | 17.5% |
| Rest of world | 455,657,174.41 | 9.6% |
| 180 records of a million acres or more | 4,585,317,938 | 96.7% |
| 52,546 records under 100 acres | 783,657 | 0.02% |

Of the US total, "BLM Lands" rows hold 254 million acres and a single "Native American Reservations" row holds 55.7 million. The counter is honest as a sum but three quarters of it is one row, which the design should acknowledge rather than hide. 989 parks have no acreage at all.

**Activities are already a clean vocabulary.** 45 distinct values from a fixed select list: Playground (33,423 parks), Picnicking, Basketball, Restrooms, Baseball, Hiking, Softball, Soccer, Tennis, Fishing, Walking, Biking, Swimming, Kayaking, Canoeing, Volleyball, Camping, Boating, Sprayground, Hunting, Horse Back Riding, Dog-Park, Horseshoes, Cross-Country Skiing, Skate Park, Disc-Golf, Golf, Water-Skiing, Handball, Ice-Skating, Roller-hockey, Snowmobiling, Sailboarding, Sailing, Backpacking, Bocce, Climbing, Archery Range, Surfing, Scuba, Skiing, Rafting, Caving, Hang-Gliding, plus one dirty value "boating,canoeing,kayaking". Average 3.0 per park; **16,264 parks have none**, mostly the 2010 bulk import.

**Ownership is free text but well-shaped.** 4,929 distinct values: 45,749 start "City of / Town of / Village of", 7,030 are counties, 6,392 "State of ...", 3,359 federal (3,313 literally "United States Government"), 4,862 other, 43 blank. This normalises into a steward taxonomy with a level (city, county, state, federal, other) almost entirely by pattern.

**The description lives in the node body.** 66,754 parks have a body; the separate `field_park_description` field is used by 7 and can be dropped. 1,601 parks have no text at all. 66,464 bodies begin with inline-styled HTML pasted from a browser or Word (`<p style="font-style: normal; ...">`), so the importer must strip styles and keep only paragraphs and links. `field_park_name` is set on 5 records; drop it.

**Images are rare.** 741 parks have at least one picture. 2,406 have a website link.

**Location is nearly complete.** 9 parks have no location, 8 have 0,0 coordinates, 1,680 have no city, 111 US parks have no state. 920 parks have two location rows attached (the second is usually a leftover) and the importer should take the first.

**URL aliases**: 66,320 follow `state/city/slug`. 1,684 have two segments and 211 one segment, which are the parks with no city or no state (waterfowl areas, conservation land, non-US). 75 contain the broken `[location-province_0]` token, 11 have no alias, and 75 US aliases carry a different state in the URL than the location record says. All of these get a new canonical URL and a redirect from the old one.

**Duplicates are few.** 42 pairs share title, city and state; 20 pairs share exact coordinates. Manageable by hand after import.

**Park Tags decode cleanly.** Stripping the city and state from each tag leaves a facility vocabulary: parks 52,310, playgrounds 31,119, basketball courts 16,481, picnic areas 14,310, baseball fields, soccer fields, trails, softball fields, tennis courts, nature preserves, hikes, volleyball courts, state parks, waterfowl production areas, dog parks, skate parks, spraygrounds, swimming pools, campgrounds, picnic shelters, wildlife management areas, boat ramps, horseshoe courts, pickleball courts, splash pads, handball courts, golf courses, disc golf courses, state forests, beaches, football fields, mountain bike trails, community gardens, roller hockey courts, bocce courts, historic sites, sledding hills, archery ranges, tot lots, BMX tracks, fishing piers and so on. About 1,086 raw variants, which collapse to roughly 80 real types once spelling variants merge (disc golf / disc-golf, roller hockey / roller-hockey, splash pads / spraygrounds). Around 11,000 tag uses start with a county name instead of the city ("Prince George's County Maryland Parks"), which is a third level, the county, worth keeping.

**Who built it and when.** Two accounts entered everything: `iceyt1981` 50,690 parks and `patrickisgreat` 17,639. 17,781 parks arrived in the 2010 import; then a quiet decade; then 3,077 in 2020, 5,846 in 2022, 9,841 in 2023, 10,521 in 2024, 7,242 in 2025 and 2,372 so far in 2026. The site is being built faster now than at any point in its life.

## What this changes in the plan

1. The acre counter has a clean numeric source: `content_type_world_parks.field_pacreage_value`, decimal. No text cleaning needed.
2. Activities are a fixed select list, not free text. The allowed values live in the field definition and come out in query 2.
3. The Park Tags vocabulary becomes the facility-type taxonomy plus redirects, which also gives the city pages that 404 today.
4. Accounts do not migrate. Votes are kept only as totals on each photo, for the historical results page.
5. The full export is small: the content tables above plus `files` on disk. No need to move 10 GB.
